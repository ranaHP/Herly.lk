# Home asset provenance and prompts

All eight PNGs were produced with the built-in image-generation tool. Hero, robot and doctor used the supplied Home screenshot as a style/content reference and were generated with actual transparent backgrounds; their alpha channels were verified. The avatar and article images were generated individually. Final files live in `assets/home`.

## home-hero.png

Extract/recreate ONLY the illustrated woman drinking from a pink heart mug at the top of the supplied reference. Long voluminous wavy dark brown hair, eyes peacefully closed, slight smile, pink top, hand holding the pale pink mug near chest. Match her pose, face and soft polished editorial illustration style closely. Include sparse pink and lavender botanical sprigs behind her shoulders and tiny pink hearts. Waist-up, centered composition, fully visible flowing hair, transparent background. No text, no UI, no phone, no extra objects. Wide 4:3 composition suitable for the upper right of an app header.

## home-robot.png

Extract/recreate ONLY the little Herly AI robot visible in the purple card of the reference. Friendly glossy pale-pink rounded robot, dark plum glass face, two pink eyes and a tiny smiling mouth, round headphones, pink heart antenna, rounded pink body. Match reference closely. Three-quarter bust, arms visible, centered, on true transparent background. Premium soft 3D illustration, no UI, no text, no backdrop. Square asset.

Icons cover period, symptoms, mood, sleep, water, exercise, nutrition, weight, self-care, more, calendar, goals, reminders, insight, steps, consultation, location, emergency, flower and sun, plus the central white petal and a botanical backdrop. These are scalable original recreations, not cropped screenshots.
